Existing CC permissions can cover training without another technology-specific copyright permission. CC0 can waive the affirmer's own covered image/privacy rights, but does not guarantee third-party rights or replace applicable data-protection requirements. The general EU TDM exception is conditional on lawful access and appropriate rights-reservation checks. An internal research-only state does not create a scientific-research exemption.
